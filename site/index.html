GPExit GitHub

Local, open source, free

Take your Google Photos home.

Import your Takeout ZIP files, fix capture dates, clean up duplicates and blurry shots, and create encrypted annual backups. Every photo stays on your computer.

Download GPExit All platforms

macOS, Windows, and Linux

01

Import

Streams AES and ZipCrypto Takeout archives, links Google JSON metadata across ZIP files, deduplicates with SHA-256, and resumes interrupted work.

02

Correct dates

Compares filename, Google metadata, and EXIF dates. Manual corrections are written back to the JSON, so they stay portable.

03

Clean up

Finds large files, nearby shots, similar photos, and low-detail images. Nothing is preselected and removals go to recoverable trash.

04

Back up

Exports one ZIP64 archive per year, optionally encrypted with AES-256, including your review decisions.

No cloud. No account. No uploads.

GPExit is a native desktop app. Analysis runs on your machine, no models are downloaded, and passwords are never stored.
